# Parts of Speech Mastery

An interactive grammar learning tool that helps English learners identify the seven parts of speech by understanding the job each word does in a sentence.

## 🎯 Purpose

Parts of Speech Mastery is designed to turn grammar instruction into a simple, interactive learning experience.

The current version focuses on:

- Verbs
- Nouns
- Pronouns
- Prepositions
- Conjunctions
- Adverbs
- Adjectives

## 🧠 Learning Flow

The learner moves through three stages:

**Learn → Strategy → Practice**

### Learn

Students review the seven parts of speech, their meanings, and examples.

### Strategy

Students learn how to identify a word by asking:

> **What job does this word do in the sentence?**

### Practice

Students answer interactive questions, receive immediate feedback, and see their progress.

## ✨ Current Features
